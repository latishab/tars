## How to Contribute

Contributions are welcome! Please:
1. Follow the attribution guidelines in [ATTRIBUTION.md](./ATTRIBUTION.md)
2. Respect the CC-BY-NC 4.0 license terms
3. Credit previous contributors appropriately
4. Document your changes clearly

For major contributions, please open an issue first to discuss the changes.
